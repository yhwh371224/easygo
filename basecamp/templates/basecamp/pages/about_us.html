About EasyGo Airport Shuttle – Sydney Airport Transfer Specialists

EasyGo Airport Shuttle is a Sydney-based private airport transfer service specialising in group travel, family transfers, and luggage-friendly transport to and from Sydney Airport.

Our story

EasyGo Airport Shuttle started in Sydney in {{ facts.founded_year }}, offering private airport transfers in 7–13 seater vehicles for families, groups and travellers with plenty of luggage — trips that don't fit in a taxi or rideshare.

In {{ facts.paused_year }}, when COVID-19 shut down air travel, we paused the business like many small transport operators. In early {{ facts.reopened_year }} EasyGo reopened under new management, keeping the same idea: a fixed price agreed before you travel, a driver who waits for you, and a vehicle big enough for everyone and their bags.

The new management rebuilt the booking system from the ground up so that small mistakes get caught before they reach you:

  • An email confirmation as soon as your booking is made, then reminder emails as your trip gets closer — and on the day itself
  • For airport pickups, an arrival email about an hour before you land with your driver's details and the exact meeting point at your terminal, with maps
  • Automatic checks for missing details such as a flight number or contact number, so we can ask you before the day rather than at the kerb
  • Every driver gets a reminder two hours before each pickup with the pickup time and any baby or booster seats needed

The result is fewer surprises, drivers who arrive prepared, and a higher standard of service on every trip.

Since reopening we have grown from Sydney to Melbourne and the Gold Coast. EasyGo is owner-operated — the owner still drives many of the trips and answers bookings personally, which is why so many customers come back.
