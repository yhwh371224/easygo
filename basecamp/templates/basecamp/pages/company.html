How bookings are handled

  • A fixed price is confirmed before travel — no surge pricing and no charge for traffic or flight delays
  • Customers receive an email confirmation, reminder emails before the trip and on the day
  • For airport pickups, an arrival email about an hour before landing gives the driver's details and the terminal meeting point with maps
  • Missing details such as a flight number or contact number are checked automatically and requested before the travel day
  • Drivers receive a reminder two hours before each pickup with the pickup time and any child seats needed
  • Free waiting: up to 80 minutes after an international landing and 50 minutes after a domestic landing
