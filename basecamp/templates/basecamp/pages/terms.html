Children's Ages & Child Restraints

Customers travelling with children must provide the age of each child at the time of booking, regardless of whether a child seat is requested. This is especially important for children aged 7 and under, so that we can prepare the correct infant, child or booster seat.

Under Australian road rules, children under 7 must travel in an approved, correctly fitted child restraint suitable for their age and size. EasyGo reserves the right to refuse transport of any child if the required restraint is not available or child safety requirements cannot be met. Where there is any doubt about a child's age, the driver may request proof of age (e.g. passport). If proof cannot be provided and, in the driver's reasonable judgement, the child appears to require a child restraint, the driver may refuse to carry the child without one. The driver's decision on safety matters is final.

Providing accurate information about children's ages is solely the customer's responsibility. If a child is refused transport because this information was not provided or was incorrect, no refund will be payable and EasyGo will not be liable for any missed flight, alternative transport cost or other loss.

Additional, oversized or special items (including skis, surfboards, golf bags, bikes, boxes, prams, baby seats and booster seats) must be declared at the time of booking and will incur surcharges as published on our Information page. Any other large or unusual items will be carried by prior arrangement only.

Booking

Bookings must be made at least 24 hours before the scheduled pick-up time through the booking form on our website. For bookings within 24 hours of pick-up, please contact us by email or message; these are subject to availability.

Airport Pickup Waiting Policy

We closely monitor flight arrivals. Flight delays alone will not affect your booking. If passengers are significantly delayed after arrival, please contact us as soon as possible.

  • International flights: Passengers are expected to proceed through immigration, collect luggage, and make their way to the pickup point within 60 minutes of landing. Vehicles may need to depart for other scheduled services if passengers are not ready within 80 minutes of landing.
  • Domestic flights: Passengers are expected to collect luggage and make their way to the pickup point promptly after landing. For domestic flights, vehicles may need to depart for other scheduled services if passengers are not ready within 45 minutes after landing.

If the vehicle has departed because the passenger was not ready within these timeframes, the booking will be treated as a no-show and no refund will be payable. For operational efficiency, drivers will wait in the holding area and will not enter the terminal unless direct contact has been made.

Waiting & Additional Charges

For pick-ups other than airport arrivals, passengers must be ready at the scheduled pick-up time. Waiting time beyond the free waiting period, returning to the pick-up location while en route, and any redirection or additional pick-up or drop-off will incur additional charges as published on our Information page. The driver may decline such requests where they would delay other scheduled services.

Our communications with our clients will be conducted via email. It is your responsibility to regularly check your email. We will not be held liable for any issues arising from failure to check or respond to emails.

Payment

Payment must be received by the deadline stated in our booking and payment emails. Bookings that remain unpaid after that deadline may be cancelled without further notice. Card and PayPal payments may incur a processing surcharge, which is shown before you complete payment.

Deposit & Cancellation Policy

A deposit (or full payment, depending on your booking type) is required to confirm your booking. The refund amount on cancellation depends on how much notice is given prior to your scheduled pick-up time, and on the type of booking, as set out below. If a deposit was paid, refunds apply to the deposit amount; if full payment was made, refunds apply to the full amount paid.

All cancellations must be requested in writing (email), and the refund tier is determined by the time the cancellation email is sent, not when it is read or acknowledged.

Standard Bookings (individual/small group bookings not falling under Group & Charter, below)

  • More than 24 hours before pick-up: 100% refund
  • Between 12 and 24 hours before pick-up: 50% refund
  • Less than 12 hours before pick-up, or no-show: No refund. A travel credit may be offered at our discretion for genuine last-minute changes (excludes no-shows).

Group & Charter Bookings

Applies to larger group, charter, special luggage, or multi-day bookings, as reasonably determined by EasyGo based on the booking details.

These bookings require additional planning, vehicle allocation and resources, and are subject to a stricter cancellation policy:

  • More than 48 hours before pick-up: 50% refund
  • Less than 48 hours before pick-up: No refund

Force Majeure Exception

The above cancellation tiers do not apply where cancellation is due to circumstances beyond the client's control, including but not limited to flight cancellations, flight delays, or severe weather. In these cases, a full refund (of deposit or full payment, as applicable) will be provided upon submission of supporting documentation (e.g. airline cancellation notice, delay confirmation).
